Why should a business leader care about local AI?

Cloud AI is convenient. Local AI is controlled. Here's what changes when you bring AI in-house:

Security
🔒

Data sovereignty

Customer contracts, financial projections, HR data — your AI processes it all inside your infrastructure. Nothing leaks to third-party APIs.

Cost
📉

Predictable costs

Cloud AI charges per token. Those costs compound fast with real usage. Local AI runs on hardware you own — marginal cost of inference is near zero.

Performance
⚡

Zero latency

No network roundtrips to an API gateway. Responses stream directly from your GPU or CPU. Critical for real-time applications.

Automation
🏗️

AI-powered agents

With tools like OpenClaw, you can connect local models to your actual workflows — calendars, files, messages — creating autonomous assistants that act on your behalf.
